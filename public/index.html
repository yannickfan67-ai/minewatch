MineWatch P2P

Players voluntarily share a Minecraft camera directly to viewers over WebRTC.

Cloudflare handles the site, signaling and optional TURN fallback. Video normally travels peer-to-peer.

Privacy note

Direct P2P can expose network addresses to the other WebRTC peer. Broadcasters can choose relay-only mode, which requires TURN and uses relay bandwidth. Camera sharing is opt-in and always shows an active-session indicator on the publisher page.
