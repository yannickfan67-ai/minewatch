Adding a camera

Activating the camera

To publish a camera, the player must explicitly enable MineWatch sharing in the client.

For now the browser publisher can be used to test the system before the Minecraft client mod is installed.

Minecraft client / mod screenshot

When sharing starts, MineWatch allocates a Camera ID. That ID appears in the camera list and can be opened from another device.

Public camera

A public camera appears in the camera directory. An unlisted camera is only available to people with its link.
